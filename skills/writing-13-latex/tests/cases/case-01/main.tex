\begin{abstract}
We study a compact retrieval-augmented reader on a synthetic corpus and report
accuracy under a fixed budget.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
Retrieval-augmented readers combine a sparse retriever with a sequence model
\citep{lewis2020rag}. The design keeps the reader small and pushes knowledge into
an external index. A recent survey claims much larger gains than we observe
\citep{unknown2021}.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
The reader attends over the top-$k$ passages. We keep the retriever frozen and
train only the fusion layer for three epochs.
