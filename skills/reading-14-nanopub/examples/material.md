## Abstract

We test whether temperature scaling repairs the calibration of a frozen image classifier. On CIFAR-100 the method lowers expected calibration error from 11.2% to 4.8%.

## Results

On CIFAR-100, temperature scaling lowers expected calibration error from 11.2% to 4.8%. Under label noise (20%), the method still lowers the error to 6.1%. The method adds no inference cost.
